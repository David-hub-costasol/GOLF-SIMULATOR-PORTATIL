Space, set-up, rain, prices and bookings for the event golf simulator on the Costa del Sol.

Questions

Do guests need to play golf?

No. The technician shows everyone how to hit in a minute and adapts the games for beginners, children and handicap golfers.

How much space do you need?

A flat area of about 6 × 5 m with 3.5 m clear height, a standard 230 V socket within 25 m and unloading access within 50 m.

Can it go indoors?

Yes: hotel halls, marquees, warehouses and offices with enough height. Outdoors we set up on grass, terraces or courts.

What about wind or rain?

Outdoors we don't set up in strong wind or rain. If the day doesn't allow it, we find a covered space or a new date together.

Is it safe?

Shots go into an impact screen inside an enclosed bay, and the technician manages turns at all times.

Can I bring my own clubs?

Yes. We also bring right- and left-handed clubs for everyone.

How long does set-up take?

We arrive about 90 minutes before the event and pack down in about 45 minutes afterwards. Set-up time doesn't count towards your hours.

What does the price include?

4 hours of play, a technician, travel between Málaga and Sotogrande, set-up, pack-down, clubs, balls and games. Each extra hour is €150 + VAT.

How do I book?

Ask for a quote, we confirm availability and the date is held with a deposit. No payment, no booking.

Can you add my logo?

Yes, on the bay and on the screen. Ask at least 4 weeks ahead.

All we need from you

  • 6 × 5 mof flat space, indoors or outdoors
  • 3.5 mof clear height for the swing
  • 230 Va standard socket within 25 m
  • 50 mat most from where we unload

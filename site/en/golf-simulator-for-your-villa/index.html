Questions

How much space do I need?

To play with a driver, about 6 × 5 m and 3.2 m of clear height. Irons and putting need less; we help you measure.

Can it stay outside all year?

The bay handles sun and light rain, but it is best deflated and stored in strong wind or when unused for weeks. It lasts much longer that way.

How long does it take?

It depends on the option: the bay is made with your logo in about 4–8 weeks; a fixed room depends on the works.

Who installs and fixes it?

We do. We are based in Estepona: we install it, show you how to use it and help if anything goes wrong.

Can I try it first?

Yes: hire the simulator for an event or an afternoon at your home and decide afterwards.

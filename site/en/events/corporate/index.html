Questions

Do guests need to play golf?

No. The technician shows everyone how to hit in a minute and adapts the games for beginners, children and handicap golfers.

How much space do you need?

A flat area of about 6 × 5 m with 3.5 m clear height, a standard 230 V socket within 25 m and unloading access within 50 m.

Can it go indoors?

Yes: hotel halls, marquees, warehouses and offices with enough height. Outdoors we set up on grass, terraces or courts.

What about wind or rain?

Outdoors we don't set up in strong wind or rain. If the day doesn't allow it, we find a covered space or a new date together.
